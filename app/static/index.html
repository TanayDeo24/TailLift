Methodology / About
  • Query-to-head-query mapping: a tail (sparse, low-support) query is ranked against a fixed index of 12,261 well-supported head queries.
  • Lexical + semantic fusion: a Jaccard token-overlap score (preserved from the original project) and a sentence-transformer cosine similarity score are min-max normalized and blended.
  • Train-only support prior: a log-scaled popularity prior, computed only from the training split, nudges the ranking toward historically well-evidenced head queries.
  • Why the cross-encoder reranker was rejected: it was evaluated as part of the modernization ladder but scored lower than fusion alone on held-out validation and test data, and was ~5x slower — so it is not part of the deployed system.
  • Raw behavioral-log limitation: this project's underlying data has no raw timestamps, user IDs, or impression lists, so session-level behavior (reformulation, abandonment, first-click position) could not be analyzed and is not claimed anywhere.
